CITN Fellow (FCTI) ANAN (CNA) IMC / CMC Fellow Fintech CFO

With over three decades of corporate finance, strategic taxation, and enterprise advisory experience, Olayiwola Kola-Seriki leads OAC Tax with an insider’s understanding of corporate ambition and regulatory complexity. A Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (FCTI), a Certified National Accountant (CNA) with ANAN, and a Fellow of the Institute of Management Consultants (FIMC/CMC), he has structured multi-billion Naira fiscal architectures for leading commercial operators.
