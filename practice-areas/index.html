Practice Pillar 01

Corporate Taxation & Strategic Fiscal Architecture

Nigeria’s fiscal policy landscape evolves rapidly with annual Finance Acts, statutory reforms, and aggressive revenue targets from federal and state tax authorities. We build robust, legally compliant tax structures that protect profit margins while maintaining complete statutory compliance.

Our advisory moves beyond routine filing. We architect your fiscal position to utilize legitimate statutory incentives, capital allowances, Pioneer status tax holidays, and optimized double taxation relief frameworks.

  • Companies Income Tax (CIT) Optimization: Strategic classification of revenue streams, deductible business expenses, and unutilized historical loss carry-forwards.
  • Withholding Tax (WHT) Credit Management: Systematic tracking, reconciliation, and utilization of WHT credit notes against corporate tax liabilities.
  • Value Added Tax (VAT) Structural Audits: Input-output VAT reconciliations, zero-rated export handling, and eliminating double-taxation exposures across vendor tiers.
  • Transfer Pricing Compliance: Preparation of statutory Master Files, Local Files, and documentation defending related-party cross-border transactions before FIRS examiners.

Practice Pillar 02

Statutory Financial Audit & Active FIRS/LIRS Defense Shield

Arbitrary desk queries, routine tax audits, and back-duty assessment notices from the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) and State Internal Revenue Services (such as LIRS) pose existential risks to corporate liquidity when handled without specialized technical representation.

Our seasoned chartered practitioners step between our clients and tax enforcement agencies. We reconcile years of transaction ledgers, assemble forensic paper trails, and defend your statutory positions before Large Taxpayer Offices (LTO) and Tax Appeal Tribunals.

  • Pre-Audit Forensic Ledger Reviews: Proactively auditing corporate books prior to official revenue authority examinations to catch and resolve reconciliation anomalies.
  • Assessment Notice Challenge & Objections: Drafting statutory letters of objection citing legal precedent, tax circulars, and judicial interpretations under the Federal High Court.
  • WHT & VAT Back-Duty Defense: Disproving speculative revenue extrapolations and defending against unmerited 10% penalty and 21% interest charges.
  • State PAYE & Withholding Tax Audit Reconciliation: Complete multi-state staff tax reconciliation, expatriate quota compliance, and statutory clearance issuance.

Practice Pillar 03

Virtual CFO & Strategic Financial Controllership

Mid-sized commercial operators, growing technology scale-ups, and established private businesses often require high-caliber CFO leadership without the fixed overhead of a full-time executive salary.

Led by veteran corporate finance directors with deep immersion in commercial banking, corporate boardrooms, and Nigeria’s enterprise sector, our Virtual CFO service provides senior financial stewardship, cash flow architecture, and macroeconomic risk hedging.

  • Boardroom Financial Reporting & KPIs: Institutional monthly and quarterly management packs formatted for institutional investors and board audit committees.
  • Treasury Architecture & FX Risk Hedging: Structuring working capital cycles, managing commercial bank lines, and navigating foreign exchange volatility.
  • Statutory Compliance Calendar Management: Enforcing strict statutory remittance timelines for PAYE, VAT, Pension (PENCOM), NSITF, and ITF.
  • Financial Model Architecture: Scenario-based budgeting, unit-economic sensitivity testing, and multi-year growth forecasts.

Practice Pillar 04

Corporate Restructuring, M&A Due Diligence & Valuation

Acquisitions, mergers, equity investments, and joint ventures carry hidden historical tax liabilities that can devastate transaction enterprise value post-close.

OAC Tax conducts rigorous buy-side and sell-side pre-deal tax due diligence. We ring-fence historical exposures, quantify contingent tax risks, issue formal valuation certificates, and design statutory integration roadmaps.

  • Comprehensive Tax Due Diligence (TDD): Multi-year audit of target company ledgers, unresolved assessments, and contingent exposures.
  • Independent Business & Asset Valuation: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), Net Asset Value (NAV), and earnings-multiple valuations for regulatory filings and board sign-off.
  • Post-Merger Statutory Integration: Harmonizing tax identities, consolidating payroll structures, and restructuring asset titles under stamp duties relief.
